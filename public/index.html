Developer Embed Routes (TMDB ID Only)

      

        

          MOVIE EMBED
          
Embed Any Movie

          /embed/movie/{tmdb_id}
          
Pass the TMDB Movie ID. Automatically resolves IMDb ID, multi-audio tracks, subtitles, and chapter segments.

          
Example: /embed/movie/550

        


        

          TV & ANIME EMBED
          
Embed Any Series or Anime

          /embed/tv/{tmdb_id}/{season}/{episode}
          
Pass TMDB Series ID, season, and episode. Automatically enables the Episodes Drawer, Skip Intro button, and Auto-Next.

          
Example: /embed/tv/94605/1/1

        


        

          M3U8 & MP4 STREAM PROXY
          
CORS & Referer Stream Proxy

          /api/proxy?url={encoded_url}&referer={upstream}
          
Rewrites .m3u8 master/media playlists, audio tracks, and keys, or streams .mp4 with HTTP 206 Range support.

          
Mode: ?mode=manifest (Free-Tier 99% BW Saver) or ?mode=full
